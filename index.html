What's inside

Train, run, eat and track — in one free app.

Five things RepWise does for you, every day.

1 · Your plan

A program built around your week

Tell RepWise how many days you can train, which days are off-limits, how long a session can be and what equipment you have. It picks the split — Full Body, Upper / Lower, PPL and more — and lays out your week.

  • Schedules around work, class and family days
  • Gym, dumbbells or bodyweight only
  • Missed a day? Make it up within 2 days
2 · In the gym

A coach on every set

RepWise remembers last time and tells you exactly what to beat — heavier weight, or one more rep for push-ups and pull-ups. The rest timer keeps you honest: no rushing into the next set.

  • Progressive overload targets every set
  • Built-in timer for planks and holds
  • Easier versions when you can't do a move yet
  • Every exercise's history with a progress graph
3 · Cardio

Runs and walks on a live map

A full-screen map follows you while you run, with your distance, time and pace below. Pick a goal — 5 km or 30 minutes — and a run : walk format like 1:1 or 3:1, and RepWise buzzes and tells you when to switch.

  • Replay any run on the map afterwards
  • Automatic steps all day, with week / month / year totals
  • Optional route suggestions for loops and one-way runs
4 · Nutrition

Food logging that speaks Filipino

Adobo, sinigang, tapsilog, kwek-kwek — over 50 local dishes and 130 ingredients with real portions. Can't find something? Add your own ingredient straight from its nutrition label, vitamins and minerals included.

  • Custom ingredients and meals, saved for one-tap logging
  • Cooking method and oil change the calories
  • Intermittent fasting with your own eating window
5 · Progress

See it working

Your 7-day weight trend, goal finish date, body composition and lift-by-lift history. Every run is saved with its map, so you can watch where you went.

  • Weight trend that ignores daily water swings
  • Body fat from your measurements
  • Personal records for every lift

Training is a game

Every set earns XP. Climb to Arnold.

Each muscle — and your cardio — ranks up on its own. Iron is quick to leave; every tier after that is a longer climb. When you rank up, RepWise celebrates after your workout, not in the middle of it.

11 ranks to climb at once

Long-term quests — your first pull-up, a 5 km run — pay out thousands of XP.

Install in a minute

Get RepWise on your phone

  1. Download the app. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the file. When it finishes, tap RepWise.apk in your notifications or Downloads.
  3. Allow the install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  4. Open RepWise and answer a few questions — your plan is ready in under a minute.

RepWise isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a one-time warning for apps from outside the store.

  1. Download the iPhone build on a computer: RepWise-unsigned.ipa.
  2. Install Sideloadly (free, Windows or Mac) from sideloadly.io and plug in your iPhone.
  3. Drag the .ipa into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID and press Start.
  4. Trust the app: on your iPhone go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.

RepWise isn't on the App Store yet. Apps signed with a free Apple ID need re-signing every 7 days (Sideloadly can do it automatically).

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no ads and no hidden pro plan. Every feature is unlocked.

Do you collect my data?

No. There's no account, no server and no analytics. Your workouts, food log and body stats stay on your phone. The code is public on GitHub so anyone can check.

Does RepWise have AI or a live coach?

No. RepWise gives you a science-based plan, set-by-set targets and tips, but no AI and no 24/7 coach. If you want those, check out Bytalyze, endorsed by SMARTFIT.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your phone. Use Settings → Backup to export a file if you switch phones.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Tell RepWise you're new and it starts with full-body or upper / lower training, keeps the same exercises each session so you can learn them, and offers easier versions of anything you can't do yet.

Does it work without a gym?

Yes. Pick "No equipment" or "Dumbbells only" in setup and every exercise is swapped for one you can do.

Why isn't it on the Play Store or App Store?

Not yet. Until then, the Android app installs directly and the iPhone app installs with your own Apple ID.
