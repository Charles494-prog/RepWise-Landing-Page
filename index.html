New · Ultra-Health Mode

Health comes first? Turn on Ultra-Health.

One switch makes RepWise stricter, and personal. Daily limits for added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, total fat and cholesterol follow WHO, AHA and ADA guidance and adjust to your own blood pressure and blood sugar readings.

A hard added-sugar cap

A food that would push you over today’s limit simply isn’t logged, with lower-sugar swaps instead.

Sodium for every food

All 162 built-in ingredients carry sodium values; dishes add it up. Your own foods ask once, then remember.

Limits from your readings

Log blood pressure and fasting sugar. If they run high, your sodium, sugar and fat limits tighten, and it tells you why.

Smarter fasting

Eat late and tomorrow’s window opens a full fast later, with a morning heads-up. Outside your window it asks: eat now, or wait?

Bedtime-aware

Within 3 hours of bed it suggests a light, high-protein snack, or staying up 2–3 hours after a full meal.

Always know where you stand

A green, amber or red badge on Nutrition, nutrient-gap checks, extra water and a wind-down reminder.

Turn it on: flip the switch on the Modes card on Today, or go to Profile → Settings → Modes & features → Ultra-Health Mode. It’s off until you choose it, and you can turn it off any time.

New · Smarter fasting

Ate late? Tomorrow adjusts.

Meals inside your eating window never count against you. Eat after it closes and Ultra-Health moves tomorrow's opening to a full fast after that meal — just for that day — and tells you in the morning. Outside the window it asks “Eat now or wait?” and reminds you what you're low on.

  • Near bedtime: a light high-protein snack, or stay up 2–3 h after a full meal
  • Gentle nudges toward the healthiest timing: earlier, and done 3 h before bed
  • Suggestions only — your window stays the one you set
  • Every tip cites its study

No math needed

Your numbers, done for you.

Answer a few questions once. RepWise works out what your body needs and keeps it updated as your weight changes.

Auto TDEE

How many calories you burn in a day — worked out from your age, height, weight, sex and how active you are (Mifflin-St Jeor, the most accurate everyday formula).

Calorie tracker

Your daily target is set from your goal: a deficit to lose fat, a small surplus to build muscle. Log food and see exactly how much you have left.

Auto protein

The protein you need to keep and build muscle: 2 g per kg of body weight while losing fat, 1.8 g per kg while building. RepWise tracks it with every meal.

Body fat

Estimated from your BMI, age, sex and build to start. Add your waist and neck with a tape measure (plus hips for women) and RepWise switches to the US Navy tape method — accurate to about ±3% — and tracks it over time.

Install in a minute

Get RepWise on your phone

  1. Download the app. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the file. When it finishes, tap RepWise.apk in your notifications or Downloads.
  3. Allow the install. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  4. Open RepWise and answer a few questions — your plan is ready in under a minute.

RepWise isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a one-time warning for apps from outside the store.

  1. Download the iPhone build on a computer: RepWise-unsigned.ipa.
  2. Install Sideloadly (free, Windows or Mac) from sideloadly.io and plug in your iPhone.
  3. Drag the .ipa into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID and press Start.
  4. Trust the app: on your iPhone go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.

RepWise isn't on the App Store yet. Apps signed with a free Apple ID need re-signing every 7 days (Sideloadly can do it automatically).

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no ads and no hidden pro plan. Every feature is unlocked.

Do you collect my data?

No. There's no account, no server and no analytics. Your workouts, food log and body stats stay on your phone. The code is public on GitHub so anyone can check.

Does RepWise have AI or a live coach?

No. RepWise gives you a science-based plan, set-by-set targets and tips, but no AI and no 24/7 coach. If you want those, check out Bytalyze, endorsed by SMARTFIT.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored on your phone. Use Settings → Backup to export a file if you switch phones.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Tell RepWise you're new and it starts with full-body or upper / lower training, keeps the same exercises each session so you can learn them, and offers easier versions of anything you can't do yet.

Does it work without a gym?

Yes. Pick "No equipment" or "Dumbbells only" in setup and every exercise is swapped for one you can do.

Why isn't it on the Play Store or App Store?

Not yet. Until then, the Android app installs directly and the iPhone app installs with your own Apple ID.
